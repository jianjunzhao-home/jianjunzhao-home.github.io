The kinds of software I study have changed over the years, but my main interest has remained the same: understanding software and improving its quality. Today, my research focuses on quantum software engineering, where I explore methods and tools for quantum software development and quality assurance. This work builds on my earlier research in software engineering and programming languages, particularly program analysis and software testing. I also continue to work on quality assurance for AI systems.

I am a Professor in the Department of Information Science and Technology at Kyushu University, Japan, with a joint appointment at the Quantum Computing Systems Center. I received my B.E. from Tsinghua University in 1987 and my Ph.D. from Kyushu University in 1997. I previously held faculty positions at Fukuoka Institute of Technology and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and served as a Visiting Scientist at MIT and a Guest Professor at the National Institute of Informatics (NII). I continue to serve as a Visiting Professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

## Research

My current research areas include:

- **Quantum software engineering**, including program testing, analysis, and specification
- **Software testing and program analysis**, including dependence analysis and verification
- **Quality assurance for AI systems**, particularly testing and analysis of deep learning systems
