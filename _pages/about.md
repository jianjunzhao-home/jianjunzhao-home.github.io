I am a Professor in the Faculty of Information Science and Electrical Engineering at **Kyushu University**, Japan. My research interests are in software engineering and programming languages, with a focus on program analysis, software testing, debugging, and verification.

My recent research also includes **quantum software engineering** and software quality assurance for deep learning systems. I am interested in methods that help developers understand program behavior and build more reliable software.

I received my B.E. from Tsinghua University and my Ph.D. from Kyushu University.

## Research

My current research areas include:

- **Quantum software engineering**, including program testing, analysis, and specification
- **Software testing and program analysis**, including dependence analysis and verification
- **Quality assurance for AI systems**, particularly testing and analysis of deep learning systems

More details are available on the [Research](/research/) page.
