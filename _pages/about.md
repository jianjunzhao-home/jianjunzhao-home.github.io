My research focuses on understanding software and developing methods and tools to improve its quality. My main research area today is [quantum software engineering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.07047), where I work toward a systematic engineering foundation for quantum software development and quality assurance. This work builds on my longstanding research in software engineering and programming languages. I also continue to work on quality assurance for AI systems.

I am a Professor in the Faculty of Information Science and Electrical Engineering at Kyushu University, Japan, with a joint appointment at the [Quantum Computing System Center](https://qcsc.kyushu-u.ac.jp/en/). I received my B.E. from Tsinghua University in 1987 and my Ph.D. from Kyushu University in 1997. I previously served as a Lecturer (1997-2000) and an Associate Professor (2000-2005) at Fukuoka Institute of Technology, and as a Full Professor (2005-2016) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I also served as a Visiting Scientist at MIT (2002-2003) and a Guest Professor at the National Institute of Informatics (NII) (2012-2017). I continue to serve as a Visiting Professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2017-present).
